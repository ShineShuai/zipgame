What is Zip?Zip 是什么？

Zip is a path puzzle inspired by the Zip game on LinkedIn. Each board has a few numbered squares and possibly some walls. Your job is to draw a single line across the board, following four simple rules. Zip 是一款路线谜题，灵感来自 LinkedIn 上的 Zip 游戏。每块棋盘上有几个带数字的格子，有时还有一些墙。你要按四条简单的规则，画出一条贯穿棋盘的线。

1

Start at 1从 1 出发

Put your finger or mouse on the square numbered 1 and start drawing.把手指或鼠标放在数字 1 的格子上，开始画线。

2

Follow the numbers按数字顺序

Pass through 2, 3, 4… in that order, never skipping or swapping one.依次经过 2、3、4……不能跳过或颠倒。

3

Fill every square填满所有格子

Your line must visit every square exactly once and cannot cross a wall.线必须恰好经过每个格子一次，并且不能穿过墙。

4

End at the top在最大数字处结束

Your line ends on the highest number, and only once every square is filled.线止于最大的数字，且此时已填满所有格子。

Every puzzle has exactly one correct solution. 每道题都恰好只有一个正确答案。

How it works程序原理

A quick look at the ideas behind Zip, with no code.不涉及代码，简单看看 Zip 背后的思路。

How a puzzle is made题目是怎么做出来的

  1. 1

    Draw a secret route先画一条秘密路线

    The computer first draws a random winding route that visits every square once. This is the answer to the puzzle.程序先随机画出一条蜿蜒的路线，恰好经过每个格子一次。这就是这道题的答案。

  2. 2

    Place the numbers标上数字

    It drops numbers 1, 2, 3… along the route, spaced out so the puzzle feels balanced.沿着路线依次标上 1、2、3……，并让数字分布均匀，使题目看起来平衡。

  3. 3

    Add walls until one answer is left加墙，直到只剩一个答案

    It scatters a few walls, then asks the solver: is there another route that also works? If so, a wall is placed in that other route’s way. This repeats until only the secret route remains.它先随机放上一些墙，再问解题程序：还有别的路线也行得通吗？如果有，就在那条路线上加一堵墙。如此反复，直到只剩下那条秘密路线。

  4. 4

    Tidy up精简整理

    Next it tries taking each wall away again and keeps only the walls that are really needed.接着逐一尝试拿掉每堵墙，只保留真正必要的墙。

  5. 5

    Keep the neatest one择优保留

    Several versions are made this way, and the one with the fewest walls wins.按这个流程做出好几个版本，墙最少的那个胜出。

Same seed, same puzzle. Every puzzle starts from a seed number, like a recipe number. The same seed always makes the same puzzle, which is why everyone sees the same puzzles on the same day. 同一个种子，同一道题。每道题都从一个种子编号开始，就像菜谱编号。相同的种子总会做出相同的题目，所以同一天里每个人看到的题目都一样。

How a puzzle is solved题目是怎么解出来的

  1. 1

    Explore, like in a maze像走迷宫一样探索

    The solver starts at 1 and moves one square at a time, always following the numbers in order.解题程序从 1 出发，每次走一格，并始终按数字顺序前进。

  2. 2

    Step back at dead ends死路就退回

    When it gets stuck, it goes back a few steps and tries another way. This is called backtracking.走进死胡同，就退回几步换一条路再试，这叫“回溯”。

  3. 3

    Spot hopeless routes early及早放弃走不通的路

    It quickly notices when a square has been cut off or trapped, or when the remaining squares can no longer all be reached, and drops that route right away.一旦发现某个格子被孤立、被困住，或者剩下的格子已经无法全部走到，就立刻放弃这条路线。

  4. 4

    Use forced moves利用必然的走法

    If a square has only two open sides, the line has to use both. The solver applies deductions like this automatically, just as a skilled player would.如果某个格子只剩两条通路，线就必须同时经过这两条。解题程序会像高手一样自动运用这类推理。

  5. 5

    Count the answers数一数答案

    It stops as soon as it finds a second solution. Exactly one answer means the puzzle is fair. Two means the puzzle maker needs to add another wall.一旦找到第二个解就停止。恰好一个答案，说明题目公平；找到两个，出题程序就要再加一堵墙。

Sounds simple, it is not 😅听起来很简单，其实不然 😅

Zip is easy to learn but surprisingly hard for a computer. Computer scientists sort problems into families by how hard they are. Here is the maths behind it, told through Zip. The families describe what happens as boards grow without limit; for the board sizes in the game, a computer copes fine. Zip 很容易上手，对电脑来说却出乎意料地难。计算机科学家按难度把问题分成不同的“家族”。下面用 Zip 来认识背后的数学。这些家族描述的是棋盘无限变大时的情形；对游戏里的棋盘尺寸，电脑完全应付得来。

⚡ P: quick to solve⚡ P 类：解得快

A problem is in P if a computer solves it quickly, even as it grows. Sorting names: twice as many, about twice the work. 如果电脑能很快解决某个问题，且问题变大后依然很快，它就属于 P 类。给名字排序：多一倍的名字，大约多一倍的活。

In Zip: is my line correct? Walk along it and check the rules. Making a solvable puzzle is quick too. 在 Zip 里：我画的线对吗？沿线走一遍对照规则即可。出一道有解的题也很快。

✔️ NP: quick to check✔️ NP 类：验证得快

A problem is in NP if a given answer is quick to check, though finding it may not be. Jigsaw: a glance to check, hours to build. 如果一个给定的答案能很快核对，这个问题就属于 NP 类，尽管找出答案未必很快。拼图：一眼就能验收，拼起来要几小时。

In Zip: does this puzzle have a solution? The drawn line proves it, and anyone can check it in seconds. 在 Zip 里：这道题有解吗？如果有，画出的那条线就是证明，谁都能在几秒内核对。

🔄 co-NP: mirror of NP🔄 co-NP 类：NP 的镜像

A problem is in co-NP if a “no” is quick to prove but a “yes” is not. “No typos here”: one typo refutes it; a full read proves it. 如果“否”容易证明而“是”不容易，这个问题就属于 co-NP 类。“这里没有错字”：找出一个错字就能推翻，要证明它成立得逐字通读。

In Zip: is this the only solution? A second line proves “no” in seconds; “yes” means ruling out every other line. 在 Zip 里：这是唯一的解吗？再画一条线几秒就能证明“不是”；要证明“是”，得排除所有其他的线。

🏔️ NP-complete: hardest🏔️ NP 完全：最难的

A problem is NP-complete if it is among the hardest in NP: a fast method for it solves all of NP. Like a master key. 如果一个问题属于 NP 中最难的那一档，就称为 NP 完全问题：有了它的快速解法，NP 里所有问题都能快速解决。像一把万能钥匙。

In Zip: solving a puzzle. Close to the Hamiltonian path problem (visit every point once), NP-complete on grids, so Zip is expected to be too. 在 Zip 里：解题。它与哈密顿路径问题（每个点恰好经过一次）相近，后者在网格上是 NP 完全的，所以 Zip 预计也是。

🚧 NP-hard: or harder🚧 NP 难：也许更难

A problem is NP-hard if it is at least as hard as all of NP, yet its answer may be hard to check, like proving a route is shortest. 如果一个问题至少和 NP 中所有问题一样难，就称为 NP 难问题，它的答案甚至也可能难以核对，比如证明某条路线最短。

In Zip: what is the fewest walls that leave exactly one solution? Proving a count is the minimum is hard. 在 Zip 里：最少要几堵墙才能恰好只有一个解？要证明某个数量已是最少并不容易。

⚖️ DP: exactly one⚖️ DP 类：恰好一个

DP (Difference Polynomial time) combines both: an NP part and a co-NP part. A detective: one suspect fits, nobody else. DP（差多项式时间）把两者合在一起：一部分是 NP，一部分是 co-NP。侦探断案：只有一个嫌疑人吻合，其他人都不吻合。

In Zip: does this puzzle have exactly one solution? That is what makes it fair: a solution (NP), and no other (co-NP). 在 Zip 里：这道题恰好只有一个解吗？这是公平的前提：有解（NP），且没有别的解（co-NP）。

Why the puzzle maker draws the answer first: making a solvable puzzle is easy, so it plants a route and hides it behind numbers. Making a puzzle with exactly one solution is the hard part, so it keeps asking the solver “is there another route?” and adds walls until the answer is no. 为什么出题程序先画答案：做一道有解的题很容易，所以它先埋下一条路线，再用数字藏起来。做一道恰好只有一个解的题才是难点，所以它不断问解题程序“还有别的路线吗？”，并不停加墙，直到答案变成“没有”。
